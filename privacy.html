Privacy policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

gta6auto is a personal publishing tool. Its only user is the channel owner, who runs it on their own computer.

What data it accesses

  • YouTube (Google): with the owner's consent, it uploads videos and thumbnails to the owner's own channel and reads the channel's name. It uses YouTube API Services.
  • Instagram and Facebook (Meta): it publishes Reels and videos to the owner's own Instagram account and Facebook Page, and reads the Page and account IDs needed to do so.
  • TikTok: it sends videos to the owner's own TikTok account and reads the account's basic profile (display name, posting options).
  • Twitch: it reads public stream video, public chat and public clip information, the same as any viewer.

How it is stored and used

Login tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. They are used only to post the owner's own approved videos and are never sold, shared or sent to anyone other than the platform they belong to. gta6auto does not collect personal data from viewers, does not use cookies or tracking, and has no accounts for other people.

Google and YouTube

By using a YouTube account with gta6auto you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke gta6auto's access to a Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Deleting data

Revoking access on the platform, or deleting the token files from the owner's computer, removes gta6auto's access immediately. Nothing is kept anywhere else.

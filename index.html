gta6auto

The publishing tool behind NovaClipsTV, a GTA 6 highlights channel hosted by Nova, an original animated character.

What it does

gta6auto is a personal tool run by the channel owner on their own computer. It watches public Twitch streams of streamers who allow clipping, finds the moments chat reacts to, and turns them into short captioned videos with Nova's commentary. Every video is reviewed and approved by the owner before it is posted. The streamer is credited on screen and in every description.

With the owner's permission it uploads the approved videos to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok accounts. It does not post for anyone else and has no other users.

Streamers

If you are a streamer and want a clip removed, or don't want your streams clipped, open an issue here and it will be taken down.
